Walking Right in Transactions - Part 2

📖 Torah Reading

“You shall have just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin...”
- Wayyiqra 19:36

✨ Message

As we enter Erev Shabbat, we deepen our focus on the mitzvah of righteous transactions. Yesterday we looked at the foundations, but today the emphasis sharpens around consistency. YHVH does not ask us to be honest only when convenient. He calls for steady righteousness in every exchange, regardless of who stands before us.

Maimonides teaches that even the smallest measurement matters, because dishonesty corrupts the entire system. When a scale is even slightly off, it creates imbalance that multiplies across many people. In our own lives, this means checking our motives, our words, and our dealings to ensure that nothing is tilted for personal benefit.

Walking in righteousness in transactions reflects the character of YHVH Himself. When we refuse to take advantage of others, we display His heart into a world that often thrives on exploitation. In the coming days - from Yom Rishon through Yom Sheni - we will continue exploring this mitzvah, as it extends far beyond money. It touches justice, mercy, and the weight of our witness before others.
