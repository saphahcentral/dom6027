🌿 SUKKOT DAY 2

Theme: “Dwelling in His Presence - Joy and Discernment”

🤲🏻 Opening Prayer

Baruch Ata YHVH, our Elohim, Sovereign of the heavens and the earth. Thank You for bringing us to this day of the Feast of Booths. May Your presence dwell among us in truth and joy. May our hearts be open to receive Your teaching, and our lives be a dwelling place for Your glory. Baruch YHVH.

🍷 Communion

As we partake of the bread and the cup, let us remember: this is the symbol of His body given for us and His blood shed for our redemption. In this booth, in this moment, we share in His life, His covering, and His peace. And we also remember it is our betrothal to our Adon.

📖 Message

This morning, on Erev Shabbat, we stand in holy expectation. The sukkah becomes a sanctuary and a prophetic sign of HaMashiach’s coming reign. We prepare our hearts to dwell in His rest.

“My Dwelling shall be with them, and I shall be their Elohim, and they shall be My people.”
- Yeḥezqěl 37:27 (YAH Scriptures)

Shabbat invites us to know His rest, to dwell not just in booths, but in the eternal presence of YHVH. The second day of Sukkot is deeper than remembrance - it is revelation. The sukkah is fragile, yet it speaks of trust: that our protection comes not from walls of stone but from the covering Presence of YHWH.

“For in the day of evil He hides me in His booth; In the covering of His Tent He hides me; On a rock He raises me up.”
- Tehillim 27:5 (YAH Scriptures)

This reminds us that dwelling in His presence is both a refuge and a revelation.

“Dwell in booths for seven days; all who are native born in Yisra’ĕl dwell in booths, so that your generations know that I made the children of Yisra’ĕl dwell in booths when I brought them out of the land of Mitsrayim. I am YHVH your Elohim.”
- Wayyiqra 23:42-43 (YAH Scriptures)

The Feast teaches us generational trust - the memory of deliverance, but also the vision of the coming dwelling of Elohim with His people.

“He who dwells in the secret place of the Most High, Who abides under the shadow of the Almighty, He is saying of YHVH, ‘My refuge and my stronghold, My Elohim, in whom I trust!’”
- Tehillim 91:1-2 (YAH Scriptures)

Today, discernment is given to those who dwell under His shadow. Where we dwell reveals the state of our hearts.

“Then YHVH shall create over all the dwelling place of Mount Tsiyon, and over her gatherings, a cloud by day and smoke and the shining of a flaming fire by night. For over all the glory there shall be a covering, and a booth for shade in the daytime from the heat, for a place of refuge, and for a shelter from storm and rain.”
- Yeshayahu 4:5-6 (YAH Scriptures)

The cloud is His glory - His covering that teaches us how to live.
