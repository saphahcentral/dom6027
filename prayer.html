📜 QARA' EL YHVH - DAILY PRAYER GUIDE

(Calling Upon YHVH – A Structured Reference)


🌅 MORNING PRAYER (QARA BOKER)

Begin your day in YHVH’s presence.

  1. Awaken and Acknowledge YHVH:
    "I thank You, YHVH, for restoring my breath this morning; great is Your trustworthiness."
  2. Proclamation:
    "This is the day YHVH has made, let us rejoice and be glad in it!" — Tehillim 118:24
  3. Shema (Restored – English):
    "Hear, O Yisra’el: YHVH is our Elohim, YHVH is one! And you shall love YHVH your Elohim with all your heart, and with all your being, and with all your might. And these Words which I am commanding you today shall be in your heart, and you shall impress them upon your children, and shall speak of them when you sit in your house, and when you walk by the way, and when you lie down, and when you rise up." — Debarim 6:4–7
  4. Morning Scriptures to Declare:
    • "My voice You hear in the morning, O YHVH; I present myself to You in the morning, and I look up." — Tehillim 5:3
    • "Because of YHVH’s kindness we are not consumed, for His compassions have not ended. They are new every morning. Great is Your trustworthiness." — Eḵah 3:22–23
  5. Praise and Thanksgiving:
    "Barukh YHVH, who daily bears our burden, the El of our deliverance! Selah." — Tehillim 68:19
  6. Petition for the Day:
    "Teach me Your way, O YHVH; let me walk in Your truth. Unite my heart to fear Your Name." — Tehillim 86:11

  7. Psalm 91

    1 He who dwells in the secret place of the Most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.
    2 I will say of YAH, “He is my refuge and my fortress; my Elohim, in whom I trust.”
    3 Surely He shall deliver you from the snare of the fowler and from the noisome pestilence.
    4 He shall cover you with His feathers, and under His wings shall you trust; His truth shall be your shield and buckler.
    5 You shall not be afraid for the terror by night; nor for the arrow that flies by day;
    6 nor for the pestilence that walks in darkness; nor for the destruction that wastes at noonday.
    7 A thousand shall fall at your side, and ten thousand at your right hand; but it shall not come near you.
    8 Only with your eyes shall you behold and see the reward of the wicked.
    9 Because you have made YAH, which is my refuge, even the Most High, your habitation;
    10 there shall no evil befall you, neither shall any plague come near your dwelling.
    11 For He shall give His angels charge over you, to keep you in all your ways.
    12 They shall bear you up in their hands, lest you dash your foot against a stone.
    13 You shall tread upon the lion and adder: the young lion and the dragon shall you trample under feet.
    14 Because he has set his love upon Me, therefore will I deliver him: I will set him on high, because he has known My name.
    15 He shall call upon Me, and I will answer him: I will be with him in trouble; I will deliver him, and honor him.
    16 With long life will I satisfy him, and show him My salvation.

☀️ MIDDAY PRAYER (QARA NATSAH)

Pause and intercede at the day’s peak.

  1. Center Yourself Before YHVH:
    "Let my prayer be prepared before You as incense, the lifting up of my hands as the evening offering." — Tehillim 141:2
  2. Midday Scriptures:
    • "But I, I pray to You, O YHVH, at an acceptable time; O Elohim, in the greatness of Your kindness, answer me in the truth of Your deliverance." — Tehillim 69:13
    • "Daniel… knelt on his knees three times a day, and prayed and gave thanks before his Elohim." — Dani’ĕl 6:10
  3. Prayer of Rededication:
    "Create in me a clean heart, O Elohim, and renew a steadfast spirit within me." — Tehillim 51:10
  4. Intercession:
    • The scattered tribes of Yisra’el
    • Those bound in spiritual darkness
    • Family, leaders, and the nations
  5. Declaration of Trust:
    "In Elohim I have trusted; I do not fear. What could man do to me?" — Tehillim 56:11

🌙 EVENING PRAYER (QARA NESHEPH)

Return your thoughts to YHVH and rest in Him.

  1. Evening Reflection:
    "I remember You on my bed; I meditate on You in the night watches." — Tehillim 63:6
  2. Evening Scriptures:
    • "In peace I lie down and sleep, for You alone, O YHVH, make me dwell in safety." — Tehillim 4:8
    • "You do not need to be afraid of sudden dread, nor of the ruin of the wicked when it comes." — Mishlĕ 3:25
  3. Confession and Covering:
    "Search me, O El, and know my heart; try me, and know my thoughts. See if there is any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting." — Tehillim 139:23–24
  4. Shema Again:
    "Hear, O Yisra’el: YHVH is our Elohim, YHVH is one! And you shall love YHVH your Elohim with all your heart, and with all your being, and with all your might. And these Words which I am commanding you today shall be in your heart, and you shall impress them upon your children, and shall speak of them when you sit in your house, and when you walk by the way, and when you lie down, and when you rise up." — Debarim 6:4–7
  5. Closing Praise:
    "Barukh are You, YHVH, who gives rest to His beloved in peace."

🕊️ DAILY REMINDERS

  • Pray aloud when possible, but always with sincerity.
  • Cover your head and arms.
  • Pause and listen - QARA’ is a conversation.
  • Let this become a rhythm, not a ritual.

Barukh YHVH. He hears when we QARA’.

“Call upon Me in the day of distress – let Me rescue you, and you esteem Me.” — Tehillim 50:15
